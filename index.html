dafoePosting

dafoePosting is a personal tool that turns text posts and images into short vertical videos (Reels) and publishes them to the author's own social media accounts, including TikTok and Instagram.

How it uses TikTok

  • Login Kit (user.info.basic): the account owner signs in with TikTok to authorize the app.
  • Content Posting API (video.upload): the app uploads a prepared video to the authorized account as a draft, which the account owner reviews and publishes in TikTok.

The app does not collect data from other users, show ads, or sell any data.
