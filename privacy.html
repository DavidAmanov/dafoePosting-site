Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

1. What we collect

When you sign in with TikTok, the app receives:

  • Your TikTok OAuth access and refresh tokens.
  • Basic profile information allowed by the user.info.basic scope (your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar).

The app does not collect any other personal data.

2. How we use it

The data is used only to identify the authorized account and to upload videos to it through the TikTok Content Posting API on your request.

3. Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored on the author's own computer or server and are not shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party, except TikTok itself as needed to perform the actions you authorize.

4. Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the authorization is active. You can revoke access in TikTok settings (Apps and websites), or ask for deletion of all stored data by emailing the address below; we will delete it promptly.

5. Children

The app is not intended for users under 13.
